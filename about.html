About Harf & Nuqta

Bridging historical scholarship and modern digital publishing to deliver authentic political literature.

Official Publisher: Dust On Books, Peshawar
Our Mission: At Harf & Nuqta (harfnuqta.store), our mission is to make scholarly, verified political biographies and constitutional history literature directly accessible to readers, researchers, and students across Pakistan and worldwide in pristine digital formats.

1. Who We Are

Founded as the digital publishing division of Dust On Books, Harf & Nuqta represents a modern approach to serious Urdu and English political literature. Based in Peshawar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, we combine rigorous archival documentation with state-of-the-art digital book typography to create reading experiences that respect both the scholarly weight of the subject and modern mobile reading habits.

2. Our Flagship Publication: Iqtidaar Ke Paar (اقتدار کے پار)

Our featured publication, "Iqtidaar Ke Paar: Hikmat, Siyasat, Muzahamat" (and its unabridged English edition "Beyond Power: Wisdom, Politics, Resistance"), represents over two years of intensive archival research, personal interviews, and political analysis.

  • Authors: Rasheed Yousafzai & Muhammad Amin Asad
  • Registered ISBN: 978-627-7650-25-4
  • Scope: 325 unabridged pages documenting 40 years of Pakistani parliamentary history, Maulana Fazlur Rehman's leadership, the 18th and 26th Constitutional Amendments, and behind-the-scenes statecraft.
  • Format: High-definition vector PDF with dual Urdu and English unabridged editions.

3. Why Digital PDF Publishing?

While printed books hold enduring cultural value, readers across the diaspora — in the UK, North America, the Gulf, and across Pakistan — often face immense shipping delays or counterfeit, poor-quality unauthorized reproductions.

By publishing official, author-verified digital editions, we guarantee:

  • 100% Unabridged Text: Direct from the authors' finalized master proofs.
  • Crisp Vector Typography: Clear Noto Nastaliq Urdu and modern Latin typography that never blurs on high-resolution screens.
  • Universal Compatibility: Seamless reading across Android, iPhone, iPad, Windows, macOS, and e-readers.
  • Instant Worldwide Delivery: Delivery via WhatsApp and email within minutes of ordering.
